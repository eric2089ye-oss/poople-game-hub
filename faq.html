PoopleGame — FAQ

Is PoopleGame official?

No. It is an independent fan resource, not affiliated with the game publisher.

Is it free?

Yes, completely free and usable without an account.

When is it updated?

Once a day, after the official reset. The archive is permanent.

Can I use it on mobile?

Yes — the layout is responsive and readable on phones.

Where do I start?

Visit pooplegame.fun for today's page.
