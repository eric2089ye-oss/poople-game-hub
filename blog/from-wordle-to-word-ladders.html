From Wordle to word ladders: why the format keeps returning

Word games spread because they share one property: the rules fit in a sentence and the puzzle changes daily.

Word ladders add a spatial twist. Instead of guessing letters in place, you move through a graph of words, one letter at a time.

That graph structure makes the format feel navigational rather than interrogative, which is why players describe solving a ladder as a route, not an answer.
