Hint design for word puzzles: what makes a clue useful

A useful clue splits the remaining possibilities roughly in half. A clever clue feels satisfying but may barely narrow anything.

Ordering matters: broad clues first (word category, starting letter), specific clues later. Revealing a specific letter too early collapses the puzzle.

The best hint pages also state when the puzzle resets and where the archive lives, so the reader knows exactly when to come back.
